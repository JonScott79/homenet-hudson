Managed IT & Network Services.

Your technology, handled.

What is it?

Managed IT & Network Services provide comprehensive monitoring, maintenance, and support for your business technology. Instead of waiting for things to break, we proactively manage your systems to ensure they run smoothly, securely, and efficiently around the clock.

Who is it for?

This service is designed for small to medium-sized businesses throughout Massachusetts, with a special focus on Hudson, the MetroWest area, and Greater Boston. If your business relies on technology to operate and you can't afford unexpected downtime, this is for you.

What problem does it solve?

It removes the headache of managing tech so you can focus on running your business. We prevent costly downtime, secure your sensitive data against modern threats, and eliminate the frustration of unpredictable IT emergencies.

What does LANZAR actually do?

  • 24/7 Network Monitoring: Keeping an eye on your connectivity and resolving issues before you even notice them.
  • Proactive Maintenance: Regular software updates and system patches to keep performance optimized.
  • Workstation Support: Helping your team with day-to-day computer issues so they stay productive.
  • Security Hardening: Implementing firewalls, antivirus, and best practices to protect your data.
  • Cloud Backups: Ensuring your critical business data is safely backed up and recoverable.
  • Responsive Helpdesk: Quick, reliable support when you or your team need it most.

Why LANZAR?

We are a veteran-owned business built on real relationships. When you call us, you get real people—like Jon and Hank—not an overseas call center. We pride ourselves on clear communication, fast response times in the MetroWest area, and honest, straightforward service without the jargon.
